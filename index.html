Imagination, backed by engineering.

I’m Abhiyuday, a developer and computer science student at San Francisco State. I left home in India to pursue opportunities that would challenge me, expand what I’m capable of, and give me the chance to turn ideas into useful software.

I like getting to the foundation of a problem: questioning assumptions, breaking it into smaller parts, and understanding why something works before deciding how to improve it. Debugging is a part of development I’m drawn to. I trace unexpected behavior back to its cause, test my reasoning, and think through the edge cases that could break a solution.

I keep building because I keep finding things worth solving: tools that make everyday work easier, applications that bring people together, and ways to make difficult concepts easier to understand. I’m interested in the whole path from an idea to a working system—the interface, the logic, the data, and how they hold up together. In a world increasingly shaped by software, I see coding ability and imagination as capabilities I can keep expanding.

Leadership has taught me to take responsibility, communicate clearly, and help people move toward a shared goal. I bring that same commitment to engineering: stay curious, work through uncertainty, and follow through. Graduating in 2028, I’m seeking a Summer 2027 software engineering internship where I can contribute that mindset, deepen my technical judgment, and take on problems that stretch me.

CourtOps

Built from my experience as an intramural sports supervisor: one workspace for game-day matchups, referee crews, results, and performance feedback across multiple sports.
